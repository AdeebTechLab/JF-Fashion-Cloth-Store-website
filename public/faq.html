FAQs

How do I place an order?

Browse the shop, add items to your cart, and tap Checkout — you'll be connected with us directly on WhatsApp to confirm sizing, delivery details, and payment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently confirm payment details directly with you over WhatsApp when your order is placed. Cash on delivery may be available depending on your city — just ask when you check out.

How can I track my order?

Since orders are confirmed and coordinated over WhatsApp, the fastest way to check your order status is to message us on the Contact page with your name and order details.

What is your return policy?

Unworn items with original tags can be returned within 7 days of delivery. See our full Returns policy for details.

Do you ship outside Bahawalpur?

Yes, we deliver across Pakistan. See our Shipping policy for estimated delivery times and costs.

How do I know my size?

Each product page lists available sizes. If you're unsure, message us on WhatsApp with your measurements and we'll help you pick the right fit.

Still have questions? Reach out on our Contact page.
